# ACME-14 Escalate a case to Tier 2

**Story:** As a support agent, I want to escalate a case to Tier 2 with a reason, so a supervisor picks it up without a hallway conversation.

## Acceptance criteria

1. On a Support Request, a support agent clicks Escalate, must pick an Escalation Reason, and the case's owner becomes the Tier 2 Support queue.
2. The escalated case shows in the supervisor's "Tier 2 Escalations" list view.
3. The supervisor clicks Accept on the case and becomes its owner.

## Story Test Steps

- Escalate a case as an agent, then take it as a supervisor.
