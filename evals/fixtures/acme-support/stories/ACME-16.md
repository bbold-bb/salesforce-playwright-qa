# ACME-16 Product Area on support requests

**Story:** As a support agent, I want to tag each Support Request with the product area it's about, so cases reach the right specialists.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A support agent can pick a Product Area from Hardware, Software, Billing and Accounts when creating a Support Request.
2. A Support Request can't be saved without a Product Area.

## Story Test Steps

- Create a case with each kind of product area.
- Try to save without one.
